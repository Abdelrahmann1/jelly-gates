Jelly Gates – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Jelly Gates ("the game", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. The game does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts.

1. Information stored on your device

Your saved game (current level, coins, boosters) and settings (music and sound on/off) are kept only on your own device. We have no servers of our own and the saved game is never uploaded. Uninstalling the game deletes it. The play statistics described in section 3 are separate from the saved game.

You can also read this policy inside the game: open Settings and tap "Privacy & Licenses". That screen also lists the open-source licenses used by the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data (for example, which ads were shown or tapped, crashes related to ads).

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own policies. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Some ads are optional "rewarded" videos that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward.

3. Play statistics (GameAnalytics)

To see which levels are too easy or too hard and to improve the game, the game can send play statistics to GameAnalytics, an analytics service that processes them on our behalf. The statistics are:

  • a random ID created by the game on your device (it is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your name or to any account),
  • device and app information (phone model, operating system version, game version),
  • what happens in the game: levels started, won and lost, time left, boosters used, coins earned and spent, and which rewarded videos and ads were shown,
  • when a play session starts and how long it lasts.

The statistics do not include your name, email, contacts or advertising ID. GameAnalytics also receives the IP address of your connection, uses it to work out your approximate location (country, region, city) and does not keep the full address with the statistics. GameAnalytics does not sell the statistics; it may use combined, de-identified statistics from many games for industry benchmarks. It keeps event data for up to 12 months. Learn more: GameAnalytics Privacy Notice.

Your choices about play statistics:

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks you first and sends play statistics only if you tap "Allow".
  • Everywhere: you can turn play statistics on or off at any time in the game, in Settings → "Play Stats". Turning them off stops the sending at once and deletes any statistics still waiting on your device.
  • Deletion: to have statistics that were already sent deleted, email us your play statistics ID. It is shown in the game under Settings → "Privacy & Licenses", right after this policy.

4. Your consent and choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before personalized ads are shown, the game displays Google's consent form so you can accept or refuse. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings or by reinstalling the game.
  • Personalized ads on Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • US state privacy laws: you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising using the device settings above.

5. Children

Jelly Gates is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads in the game are limited to content rated PG (parental guidance) or lower.

6. In-app purchases

If in-app purchases are offered (for example coins or removing ads), payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details.

7. Data sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising data handled by Google AdMob and the play statistics handled by GameAnalytics, both described above, the game does not send personal information to third parties. Data sent to these services is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
