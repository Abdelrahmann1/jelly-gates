How to play

Easy to learn in seconds, hard to master.

  1. Slide

    Drag a jelly to slide it across the board. Move the blockers first.

  2. Match

    Guide each jelly to the gate on the edge that matches its color.

  3. Escape

    Clear every jelly from the board before the timer runs out.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Jelly Gates works offline, so you can play on a flight, on the train or at home.

Is the game free?

Yes. It is free to play and contains ads. Short ads may appear between some levels, and rewarded videos are always optional.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your own device. There is no account to create, and uninstalling the game deletes your progress.

I am stuck on a level. What can I do?

Use Freeze to stop the timer, or Smash to break one jelly that blocks the way. You earn boosters by beating Hard Levels, with coins, or by watching an optional short video.
